My Schedule & Pay Stub Check

Support

For individual shift workers who want to compare their own schedule information with their own pay stub.

What the app does

  • Create a pay period with employer, currency, date range, and time zone.
  • Import your own schedule or pay-stub evidence from a photo, the camera, or a readable PDF.
  • Review and confirm recognized fields before they are saved.
  • Confirm your own pay rules and actual work time, then compare supported gross-pay and hour values.
  • Keep versioned discrepancy evidence and mark issues as explained or corrected.

Supported files

Photos and readable, unencrypted PDF files are supported. PDF imports are limited to 25 MB and 100 pages. The app intentionally asks you to select the relevant personal region before saving evidence.

Purchase model

The first successful pay-stub comparison is free. After that, a one-time in-app purchase unlocks additional comparisons and discrepancy-evidence export. It is not a subscription. Purchases and restoration are handled by Apple StoreKit.

Important limits

The app is a personal record and comparison tool. Recognition results are candidates that you must verify. The app does not provide payroll processing, tax calculation, legal advice, labor-law determinations, or a guarantee that an employer's payment is legally correct.

Troubleshooting

  • If a PDF cannot be imported, confirm it is readable, unencrypted, and within the size/page limits.
  • If text recognition is incomplete, crop to the relevant area and confirm the values manually.
  • If a prior purchase is not recognized, use Restore Purchase on the unlock screen while signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase.
