My Schedule & Pay Stub Check

Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Summary

My Schedule & Pay Stub Check is designed as a local-first personal utility. Schedule and pay-stub content is processed on your device. The app does not require an account, does not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not upload your schedule, pay-stub images, PDFs, recognized text, or comparison records to a developer-operated server.

Data you choose to use

The app can work with photos, camera captures, readable PDFs, confirmed schedule entries, confirmed pay-stub fields, pay rules, actual work time, comparison results, and discrepancy evidence. These items are used to provide the functions you request and are stored in the app's local container on your device.

Photos, camera, and files

The app requests access only when you choose an import or capture action. Recognition is performed with Apple on-device frameworks. Before evidence is saved, you choose the relevant personal region and confirm the resulting values. The developer does not receive the source document or recognized text.

Purchases

A one-time in-app purchase is processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple may process transaction and account information under Apple's own privacy practices. The app checks Apple-provided purchase entitlement information to determine whether paid features are unlocked. The developer does not receive your full payment-card information.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The app contains no developer-operated analytics service, advertising SDK, or cross-app tracking mechanism.

Sharing

The app does not sell personal data and does not transmit your schedule or pay-stub documents to third parties. If you explicitly export or share evidence using the iOS share sheet, you choose the destination and the receiving app or service then handles that copy under its own terms.

Retention and deletion

App data remains on your device until you delete it through available app actions, remove the app, or otherwise erase the device/container. Apple purchase history may remain associated with your Apple Account so that eligible purchases can be restored.

Children

The app is not designed as a children's service and does not knowingly operate an account or cloud-data service for children.

Contact

Privacy questions: fivechaoice@gmail.com

Please do not email unredacted pay stubs, banking information, government IDs, or other sensitive documents.
